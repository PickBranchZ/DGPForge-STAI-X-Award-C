# Statistical Methods And Safeguards

DGPForge reports simulation-study evidence under configured synthetic causal DGPs. It evaluates estimators under known causal truth generated from the configured DGP. This is simulation validation, not real-world causal identification. Unsupported uncertainty is left blank rather than filled with approximate intervals that are not implemented for that module.

Optional LLM-assisted commands do not change any method validity claim. They can draft, extract, explain, or review DGP contracts, but truth, simulations, estimator performance, coverage, diagnostics, and reports are produced only by deterministic DGPForge modules after schema validation.

| Module | Estimand | Truth engine | SE/CI status | Coverage status | Caveat / safeguard |

| Count/rate outcome | Marginal rate difference primary target; rate ratio and log rate ratio reported | Oracle rates `sum(mu_a) / sum(exposure)` | Naive rate difference has an independent Poisson rate SE when overdispersion is disabled; offset g-computation and IPW intervals remain blank | Reported only for rows with finite intervals | Exposure offsets define denominators. Unsupported count/rate SEs are blank rather than fabricated. |
| Missing data | Full-data causal estimand under observed outcome masking | Same truth as the full-data DGP because missingness is applied after potential outcomes are generated | Complete-case intervals reuse underlying estimator intervals; missingness-IPW OLS point estimate currently has blank SE/CI | Reported only where intervals are finite | MCAR/MAR/MNAR are configured mechanisms, not inferred from data. MNAR is a stress test unless an identifying strategy is added. |

## Shared Safeguards

- No official STAI-X data or external real datasets are included.
- DGPForge does not perform causal discovery, arbitrary DAG identification, do-calculus, or proof of real-world causal validity.
- The from-text parser and LLM-assisted from-paper workflow record assumptions; deterministic modules produce the evidence.
- MCSE columns describe finite Monte Carlo precision of summaries, not single-dataset estimator uncertainty.
- "Verified" means deterministic contract checks and known-truth Monte Carlo validation under the configured DGP.
