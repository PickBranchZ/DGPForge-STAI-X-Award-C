# Causal DGP Contract

DGPForge is causal because the contract specifies a data-generating process with treatment, potential outcomes, known counterfactual truth, and estimator stress tests. It is causal-structure-aware simulation, not causal discovery.

## What Is Causal In DGPForge?

A DGPForge contract defines the causal world before data are generated:

- covariates and roles, including confounder, prognostic variable, effect modifier, latent `U`, and cluster effect;
- treatment assignment mechanism;
- outcome model under treatment and control;
- estimand and contrast;
- sample sizes, replications, seed, diagnostics, and estimators.

The simulator then generates potential outcomes and observed data from that contract. Because the data-generating process is known by construction, DGPForge can compute the causal target and compare estimators to that truth.

## Structural Equations

The supported cross-sectional templates follow this compact structure:

```text
X -> A
X, A -> Y
U -> A, Y
effect modifier -> treatment effect
cluster/random effect -> treatment or outcome structure
missingness mechanism -> observed data
```

Observed covariates can affect treatment assignment and/or outcomes. A latent `U` can be generated by the DGP and hidden from observed estimators for sensitivity studies. Effect modifiers change individual treatment effects. Cluster effects can enter treatment or outcome structure. Missingness is applied after full potential outcomes are generated so the full-data truth remains available.

## Potential Outcomes

DGPForge generates counterfactual quantities before constructing observed outcomes:

- continuous outcomes: `Y(0)`, `Y(1)`, individual treatment effects, and observed `Y`;
- binary outcomes: oracle probabilities `p0`/`p1`, binary `Y0`/`Y1`, and observed `Y`;
- count/rate outcomes: exposure denominators and expected counts `mu0`/`mu1`.

The observed outcome is selected by treatment assignment, while the truth engine keeps access to the configured counterfactual quantities.

## Supported Estimands

DGPForge currently supports known marginal causal targets used by the reports:

- marginal ATE;
- risk difference;
- risk ratio;
- marginal odds ratio;
- rate difference;
- rate ratio.

These are simulation estimands under the configured DGP, not real-world causal identification claims.

## What DGPForge Does Not Claim

DGPForge does not perform:

- causal discovery;
- arbitrary DAG identification;
- do-calculus;
- proof of real-world causal validity;
- inference from official STAI-X data or external real datasets;
- general paper reproduction.

The intended use is narrower: build a known-truth causal DGP, run estimators and diagnostics under that DGP, and report where they succeed, fail, or lack supported uncertainty calculations.
