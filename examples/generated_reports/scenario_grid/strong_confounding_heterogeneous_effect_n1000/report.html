DGPForge known-truth simulation report

Strong Confounding Heterogeneous Effect N1000

Synthetic-data tools generate data that look real. DGPForge generates data whose truth is known, then asks whether candidate estimators recover that truth.

How to read this report Bias and RMSE are computed against the known ATE. Coverage is the fraction of Monte Carlo intervals containing that truth. The SEs are lightweight model or influence-style approximations for benchmarking, not a full inferential audit.

Known-Truth Workflow

1
DGP contractYAML defines covariates, treatment, outcome, estimand, and seed.
2
Potential outcomesSimulator generates Y0, Y1, treatment, observed Y, and true propensity.
3
Known targetATE is computed from the configured potential-outcome model.
4
BenchmarksNaive, adjusted OLS, IPW, AIPW, and optional misspecification variants target the same estimand.
5
DiagnosticsBias, RMSE, coverage, MCSE, overlap, and warnings close the loop.
